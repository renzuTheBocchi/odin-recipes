Lasagna

Description

A classic Italian baked pasta dish made of wide, flat pasta sheets stacked in alternating layers with savory fillings and sauces.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets: 12 to 15 standard, no-boil, or fresh noodles.
  • Ground meat: 1 lb lean ground beef, or a mix of ground beef and Italian sausage.
  • Yellow onion: 1 medium, finely diced.
  • Garlic cloves: 2 to 3, minced.
  • Olive oil: 1 tbsp for sautéing.
  • Tomato sauce/Marinara: 24 to 28 oz of crushed tomatoes or prepared marinara sauce.
  • Tomato paste: 2 to 3 tbsp to deepen the flavor.
  • Dried herbs: 1 tbsp Italian seasoning (or a mix of dried basil and oregano).
  • Sugar: 1 pinch to balance the tomato acidity.
  • Salt and black pepper: To taste.
  • Ricotta cheese: 15 to 16 oz (or cottage cheese as a substitute).
  • Mozzarella cheese: 3 to 4 cups (12 to 16 oz), shredded and divided.
  • Parmesan cheese: 1/2 to 3/4 cup, freshly grated.
  • Egg: 1 large, to bind the cheese layer.
  • Fresh parsley: 2 tbsp, finely chopped.

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the meat and aromatics: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, add the diced onion and minced garlic, and cook until softened. Add the ground meat and brown it completely, breaking it apart as it cooks, then drain any excess fat.
  3. Simmer the sauce: Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, sugar, salt, and black pepper. Lower the heat and let the sauce simmer for 15 to 20 minutes to develop the flavors.
  4. Mix the cheese layer: In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, fresh parsley, and a pinch of salt. Mix well until smooth.
  5. Boil the noodles (if required): If using standard lasagna sheets, boil them according to the package instructions until al dente, then drain and set aside. Skip this step if using no-boil sheets.
  6. Begin assembly (Layer 1): Spread a thin layer of the meat sauce across the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish, then arrange a single layer of noodles over the sauce.
  7. Add cheese and repeat (Layers 2+): Spread a portion of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, sprinkle with shredded mozzarella and grated Parmesan, and top with another layer of meat sauce. Repeat this noodle-ricotta-mozzarella-sauce pattern until all components are used, ending with a final layer of noodles topped with meat sauce.
  8. Add the cheese topping: Generously sprinkle the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese over the top layer of sauce.
  9. Bake covered: Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil (tenting it slightly so it does not touch the cheese) and bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Bake uncovered and brown: Remove the aluminum foil and bake for an additional 15 to 20 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown on top.
  11. Rest and serve: Remove the lasagna from the oven and let it rest for 15 to 20 minutes before slicing so the layers hold their shape.
